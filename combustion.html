What Combustion Optimization Delivers

Peak Efficiency. Flame Stability. Regulatory Compliance. Simultaneously.

Achieving all three simultaneously — across multiple fuel types, variable loads, and shifting ambient conditions — is beyond the sustained attention span of any human operator. DES combustion control achieves it automatically, without operator intervention, at every operating point your facility encounters.

01

Excess Air Elimination

Every BTU of fuel burned with more air than stoichiometry requires is a BTU you paid for and didn't convert to useful heat. DES combustion control continuously trims excess air to the minimum safe margin — maximizing thermal efficiency while maintaining the safety buffer your process requires. The savings are not dramatic on any given minute. They are relentless across every operating hour.

02

Multi-Fuel Combustion Control

Biomass, natural gas, fuel oil, and waste-heat configurations present fundamentally different combustion challenges. DES combustion control manages all of them simultaneously — applying fuel-specific optimization strategies that account for the unique combustion dynamics, calorific variability, and constraint profiles of each fuel stream.

03

Flame Stability Management

Combustion instability introduces both cost and risk. DES control maintains flame stability across variable loads and fuel compositions — eliminating the trips, startups, and manual interventions that instability causes, and the wear that comes with them.

04

Continuous Regulatory Compliance

Opacity, O2 percentage, NOx, and particulate emissions are monitored and managed continuously, not periodically reviewed. Regulatory limits become constraints that the system respects automatically, eliminating the compliance exposure that manual monitoring inevitably creates.
