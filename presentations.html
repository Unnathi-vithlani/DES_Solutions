Official Abstract Title (SME Conference Programme)

"Automated Control Solutions Dynamically Respond to Changing Steam and Electrical Demands"

Abstract

This presentation documents field-validated results from DES automated control deployments across multiple industrial facilities in North America — demonstrating how closed-loop autonomous control responds in real time to changing steam and electrical demands without operator intervention. Case studies include pulp and paper, utility power stations, and oil sands applications where DES MultiMaxx and OptiMaxx technology delivered measurable, sustained, and independently verifiable cost reductions. Awarded SME Innovation Game Changer recognition for technology that predictively models future steam demand and acts ahead of the curve.
