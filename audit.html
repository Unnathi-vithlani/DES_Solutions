The DES Audit Process

Precise Enough to Fund a Business Case.
Accurate Enough to Hold Us Accountable.

Most technology vendors provide projections based on industry averages or analogous case studies. DES provides documented analysis of your facility — because two decades of field deployments have calibrated our audit methodology to a level of accuracy that generic models cannot approach.

01

Facility Data Collection & Process Analysis

DES engineers review your facility's historical operating data, energy cost structure, control system architecture, and current constraint inventory. This is not a checklist exercise. It is an engineering analysis conducted by professionals who have operated processes identical to yours and who know where the value is before they read the first data file.

02

Opportunity Identification & Quantification

Every optimization opportunity is identified and quantified in documented financial terms — specific dollar figures, tied to specific process variables, at your specific facility. The analysis covers fuel cost reduction, electrical cost optimization, production cost improvement, and equipment life extension. There are no ranges. There are numbers.

03

Business Case Development

DES provides the complete business case your organization needs to move from interest to capital approval, including ROI projections, payback periods, implementation timelines, and risk assessments. This document is designed to survive executive scrutiny, because it is based on the same methodology that produced the documented results DES clients reference.

04

Post-Deployment Accountability

The audit projection is not the end of DES's responsibility — it is the beginning. After deployment, DES tracks actual results against projected results and reports the variance. DES's plain-language constraint messaging system automatically identifies what is preventing the system from doing better and that question, "What prevented us from doing better?", is the question DES asks every day on behalf of its clients.
