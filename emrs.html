What EMRS Does

Continuous Optimization. Zero Lapses. Every Constraint Observed.

Most energy management systems generate reports. Some generate recommendations. EMRS generates results — by acting autonomously on every economic opportunity your facility presents, within every constraint your process imposes, at every moment of every day.

01

Fuel Allocation Optimization

EMRS continuously calculates the lowest-cost fuel combination that meets your steam demand — switching between biomass, natural gas, fuel oil, and waste fuels based on real-time pricing and availability, while observing every environmental and operational constraint. No operator can do this continuously across an entire shift. EMRS does it every second without fatigue.

02

Boiler Loading & Steam Distribution

Across multiple boilers with different efficiency curves, fuel capabilities, and constraint profiles, EMRS determines the optimal loading distribution in real time — ensuring steam is always generated at the lowest marginal cost regardless of demand fluctuations, maintenance windows, or process upsets.

03

Constraint Management & Reporting

EMRS observes hundreds of simultaneous constraints — process limits, safety interlocks, environmental thresholds, equipment limits, and contractual obligations, without ever treating them as obstacles to optimization. When a constraint is binding, EMRS informs operators in plain language what it is and what equipment improvement would unlock additional savings. Daily constraint frequency reports create a continuously updated capital improvement roadmap.

04

Autonomous Action, Not Advisory Output

EMRS does not surface recommendations for operators to evaluate and implement. It acts directly, making and executing optimization decisions faster than any human operator can respond to the data that triggered them. Operators become managers of the process rather than real-time responders to it.
