SME 2012 PRESENTATION SLIDES
=============================

Folder: images/sme2012/

Expected files: img0.jpg, img1.jpg, img2.jpg ... imgN.jpg

These are the slide images from the DES 2012 SME Annual Conference presentation:
"Automated Control Solutions Dynamically Respond to Changing Steam and Electrical Demands"
Presenter: Ron L. Childress Jr. — Dynamic Energy Systems, LLC / DES Global, LLC

HOW TO SAVE THE SLIDES FROM THE OLD SITE (before desglobal.com goes down):
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open your browser and go to:
   https://www.desglobal.com/presentations/SME%202012/img0.html

2. Right-click the slide image → "Save image as"
   Save it as: img0.jpg  (in THIS folder)

3. Then try img1.html, img2.html, img3.html ... and so on.
   Keep going until you get a 404 / page not found.
   Save each image as img1.jpg, img2.jpg, etc.

4. Once all images are saved here (img0.jpg through imgN.jpg),
   the slide viewer on presentations.html will automatically work —
   no other changes needed.

NOTE: The viewer auto-detects how many slides are present (img0.jpg, img1.jpg...)
and enables Prev/Next navigation accordingly.
